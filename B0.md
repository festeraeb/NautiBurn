cesarops2: `/usr/local/cuda` → `/usr/local/cuda-12.8`, and `/usr/local/cuda/bin/nvcc` is the same 12.8.93. `nvcc` is not on the default `PATH`. `/usr/local/cuda-13.0` is also installed (`nvcc` 13.0, V13.0.88).
